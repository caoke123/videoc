# Design

Concept: keep the generated creator footage feeling like a genuine phone-shot recommendation; the only editorial layer is a readable closing choice prompt using the two plush colorways as its accent.

- Canvas: vertical 720 × 1280, 24 fps, 14 seconds.
- Palette: source daylight and product colors, with warm ivory `#FFF8F0`, deep cocoa `#392C2B`, and berry `#B94667` for the closing label.
- Type: Noto Sans JP bold for the names and regular for the question; one CJK-capable family keeps the short prompt conversational and clear.
- Focal element: preserve the generated footage full frame; never crop either product or cover creator dialogue.
- Edge anchors: a low centered closing label inside the S03 lower safe area, with comfortable side margins.
- Background: source footage only. No added music, effects, logo, or promotional claims; retain the natural creator audio on each shot.
