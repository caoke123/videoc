# V2 coherent detail-page image system

## Visual bible

- Format: 1122 × 1402 vertical 4:5 modules.
- Environment: one cream perforated-leather China-market left-hand-drive sedan.
- Lighting: warm natural daylight with green exterior bokeh.
- Palette: cream, black, beige, white, muted blush pink, subtle blue cabin accent.
- Model continuity: one young adult East Asian woman, long straight dark-brown hair, white short-sleeve top, natural makeup.
- Product continuity: white and beige rabbit SKUs remain separate complete toy-and-pad assemblies.

## Page sequence

1. `01-hero-pair.png` — paired collection hero with copy space.
2. `02-beige-driver-master.png` — complete driver context and installed-state proof.
3. `03-beige-installed-closeup.png` — same woman and car, tighter product detail.
4. `04-white-driver.png` — same master scene with the white colorway.
5. `05-softness-detail.png` — non-installation tactile material demonstration.
6. `06-pair-material-packshot.png` — paired texture and catalog close.

Text, claims, price, CTA, and graphic decoration are intentionally reserved for later layout overlays rather than generated into the photographs.
